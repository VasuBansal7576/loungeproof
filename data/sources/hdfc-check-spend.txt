1. Login to Netbanking / Mobilebanking 
2. Click 'Cards' - Select Regalia Gold card 
3. Next to 'Reward Points' click on "Redeem" 
4. New window → Click Proceed 
5. Place mouse on MYREWARDS tab → Click "Spend Promo Details" 
6. Skip to last page 
7. Find “Regalia Gold Domestic Lounge” – this will show customer's eligibility 
status of domestic lounge access in next quarter

o Completed - Spends met and customer can swipe credit card for 3 
lounge access in next calendar quarter
